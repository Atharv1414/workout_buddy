# Store review notes

Workout Buddy uses the front-facing camera for on-device pose estimation. To review:

1. Open the app and complete onboarding.
2. Select Squats, Push-ups, or Bicep curls.
3. Grant camera permission and place a full body or tracked arm in frame.
4. Complete a full contracted-to-extended movement to observe repetition counting.

Camera frames are processed transiently on the device and are not recorded or uploaded.

Apple Health or Health Connect export is optional. It can be enabled under Settings → Connections. The app requests write-only workout access and continues to save sessions locally if access is declined.

Technogym is displayed as pending and cannot be connected in this release. No Technogym data or credentials are present in the app.
