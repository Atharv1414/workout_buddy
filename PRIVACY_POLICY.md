# Workout Buddy Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 August 2026

Workout Buddy is designed to provide exercise repetition counting and form cues while minimizing data collection.

## Data processed by the app

- **Camera frames:** The app processes live camera frames on your device to estimate body pose. Frames are not saved, uploaded, or shared by Workout Buddy.
- **Workout summaries:** Exercise name, repetition count, duration, and completion time are stored locally on your device so the app can show your history and totals.
- **Health data:** If you explicitly enable Apple Health or Health Connect synchronization, the app writes completed workout summaries to the health service you selected. Workout Buddy does not read your other health records. You can decline or disable this connection at any time.

Workout Buddy does not currently use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or crash-reporting SDKs. It does not sell personal information.

## Permissions

Workout Buddy requests camera access only when you start a camera-based workout. Health access is requested only when you enable health synchronization in Settings. Denying either permission does not transmit any data to Workout Buddy.

## Data sharing and third parties

When health synchronization is enabled, Apple Health or Health Connect processes the workout according to its own terms and privacy policy. Technogym synchronization is not enabled in this release and no information is sent to Technogym.

## Retention and deletion

Local workout history remains on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Disabling health synchronization stops future exports but does not delete workouts already written to Apple Health or Health Connect; those records can be managed in the applicable health app.

## Children

Workout Buddy is not directed to children under 13. A parent or guardian should supervise exercise and device use by minors.

## Changes

Material changes will be reflected in this policy and its effective date before a release that uses the changed data practice.

## Contact

For privacy questions or deletion assistance, [open a privacy request on GitHub](https://github.com/Atharv1414/workout_buddy/issues/new).
